# Veridexa (VDX) — Architecture

## Overview

Veridexa is designed as a future-oriented technology and Web3 ecosystem focused on digital identity, verification, artificial intelligence, blockchain technology, and trusted digital infrastructure.

## Core Architecture

The planned ecosystem consists of several conceptual layers:

### 1. Blockchain Layer

Ethereum Mainnet provides the underlying blockchain infrastructure for VDX.

### 2. Token Layer

VDX is an ERC-20 token designed to support potential future ecosystem utilities.

**Total Supply:** 1,000,000,000 VDX

**Decimals:** 18

### 3. Identity Layer

Future development may explore digital identity technologies and identity-related applications.

### 4. Verification Layer

Future systems may explore digital verification, trusted information, and verification services.

### 5. Intelligence Layer

Future development may explore AI technologies that can support identity and verification applications.

### 6. Application Layer

Future decentralized applications may connect users, developers, identity systems, and ecosystem services through Web3 technologies.

### 7. Community Layer

Community participation may support:

- Ecosystem development
- Feedback
- Community initiatives
- Collaboration
- Future platform adoption

## Architecture Principles

Veridexa development should prioritize:

- Privacy
- Security
- Transparency
- Simplicity
- Modularity
- Scalability
- User control

## Future Development

The architecture may evolve as new technologies, applications, and ecosystem requirements are introduced.

Planned components are not considered live until officially implemented.
